# SurvivorQoL

Quality-of-life modules for **Azeroth Survivors** (WoW 1.12). Turn each one on or off in the settings window.

Open the settings with **/qol** or the gear button on the minimap.

## Modules

- **Nameplates**: our own enemy nameplates. Bosses and guardians are bigger, your target gets a gold border, and when lots of enemies are on screen the ordinary ones shrink and fade.
- **Plate Pass-Through**: right-click turning works when the mouse is over a nameplate. Doesn't change how plates look. Hold Shift to click a nameplate.
- **Stats Panel**: your main stats during a run. When a pick changes a stat, the difference shows for a few seconds.
- **Cursor**: six styles to find your mouse on a crowded screen (Halo, Reticle, Crosshair, Spotlight, Sonar, Laser lines), plus a trail.
- **Steer Mode**: your hero turns with the mouse without holding right-click. Lets go for cards, windows, chat and Alt, and parks the cursor at an aim spot so cards are easy to click.
- **Boss Targeting**: Tab targets nearby bosses and guardians first, and can target them by itself once they are close.
- **Damage Meter**: makes the game's own damage meter bigger.

## Moving frames

On the General page, click **Move frames**, drag the frames where you want them, then click **Done**. Settings and positions are saved for your account, so every character shares them.

## Commands

- `/qol` opens or closes the settings window.
- `/qol move` starts Move frames.
- `/qol stats all` lists every stat the game sends.
- `/qol find <word>` lists shown frames with that word in their name.

## Install

Install it with SurvivorsForge, or copy the `SurvivorQoL` folder into `benilla-config\AddOns`.
